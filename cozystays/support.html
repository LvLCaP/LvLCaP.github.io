Cozy Stays – Support

Need help or found a bug? Open a support request at GitHub Issues (choose "New issue") — we usually reply within 2 business days.

FAQ

I bought something but didn't receive it. Open Shop → Restore purchases. Gem packs are delivered instantly; if not, open a support request with the date of purchase.

How do I remove ads? Buy "Remove Ads", "Starter Pack" or "VIP" in the Shop. Optional reward videos stay available.

How do I change ad consent? Shop → Privacy settings (EEA/UK), or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
