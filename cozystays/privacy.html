Cozy Stays – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Cozy Stays ("the app") is a game developed by Vitalii Huk ("we"). This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

1. Data we collect ourselves

We do not run our own servers and do not collect personal data. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location, ad interaction data and diagnostics to serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the app: Shop → Privacy settings. On iOS you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

5. Your rights

You may request information about, or deletion of, data related to you. Since we do not store personal data, deleting the app removes all game data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools linked above.

6. Contact

Open a support request: https://github.com/LvLCaP/LvLCaP.github.io/issues
